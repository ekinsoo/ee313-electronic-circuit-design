# Single-Supply Push-Pull Power Amplifier

I designed a single-supply push-pull amplifier with an LM358 and a complementary transistor output stage. I examined gain, output power into a 33-ohm load, harmonic content, quiescent power, and efficiency.

The [final report](final-report.pdf) includes the circuit drawings and breadboard measurements. It reports about 1.07 W output power and 50% efficiency at 1 kHz. These are the original lab measurements; they have not been repeated for this repository.
